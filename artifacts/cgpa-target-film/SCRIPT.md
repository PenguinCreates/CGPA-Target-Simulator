# Studyline — CGPA Target Simulator

## Director's treatment

- **Promise:** A student's future CGPA stops being a mystery and becomes a plan they can shape.
- **Identity:** Studyline's cool academic palette—mist blue, paper, ink navy, and sharp cobalt—with green/amber/red reserved for reachability. Instrument Serif is the emotional display voice; DM Sans and IBM Plex Mono carry the interface.
- **Hero:** A precise, oversized CGPA dashboard that behaves like a physical planning instrument: cards, a trajectory line, and grade tokens.
- **Motion:** Snap-set type and data; a camera-like push through the chart; a grade-token cascade that redraws the forecast.
- **Arc:** Uncertainty → record → target → what-if → graduation clarity.
- **Payoff:** The interface folds into one clean outcome statement, with a rising trajectory line becoming the Studyline mark.
- **Format:** 16:9 landscape.
- **Runtime:** 24 seconds, five scenes.

## Full shot script

### Shot 01 / 0.000–4.667s / 4.667s — “WHAT WILL IT TAKE?”

**Purpose:** Turn academic uncertainty into a tangible, measurable question.

**Exact content:** `WHAT WILL IT TAKE?` appears in two quick chunks from 0.00–0.72s. `CURRENT 3.18` appears 0.85–1.40s. `TARGET 3.60` appears 1.35–1.95s. A large `0.42` holds from 2.05–3.60s with the label `THE GAP` from 2.18s to the cut.

**Composition:** Mist-blue full field with paper-white ledger strips. Main phrase upper-left, 10vmin Instrument Serif; oversized `0.42`, 30vmin cobalt mono numerals at center-right. Tiny current and target figures occupy separate lower-left/right corners, never stacked beside the headline. Navy grid rules and an amber target tick establish a precise instrument-like space.

**Artistic idea:** The gap between current and target is drawn as a physical measuring aperture; the numeral is not a KPI card but the width of a visible cobalt band.

**Timed choreography:** 0.00: cobalt band slices across frame and lands as the decimal point of a giant `0.42`. 0.12–0.55: `WHAT / WILL IT TAKE?` snaps in as two offset lines; a thin ledger grid reveals behind it. 0.65–1.45: `3.18` and `3.60` click into opposite ends of a baseline; a cobalt ruler grows between them. 1.45–2.15: the camera tightens toward the ruler; it expands into the `0.42` figure, with a short spring overshoot. 2.15–3.65: amber tick travels along the band, stopping at the target. 3.65–4.667: the band endpoint swells into a circular chart node while the composition pushes through it.

**Transition contract:** At 4.667s, the blue chart node lands at the same center point and scale as Shot 02's first semester point. The outgoing ruler line continues across the cut; the small amber target tick remains visible over the new chart.

**Assets and technique:** Custom CSS/SVG typography, grid rules, ruler band, node, and masks. Fonts: Instrument Serif, DM Sans, IBM Plex Mono. No photography required.

**Sound intent:** Instrumental lo-fi electronic bed; no VO or SFX requested. The designated handoff at 4.667s aligns to a measured onset in the locally synthesized bed.

### Shot 02 / 4.667–9.333s / 4.666s — “THE RECORD, MADE LEGIBLE.”

**Purpose:** Show that completed semesters and actual course grades form the trustworthy starting point.

**Exact content:** Heading `COMPLETED SEMESTERS` 4.68–6.50s; term labels `SEM 01` and `SEM 02` 5.05s onward; course labels `BIOLOGY`, `WRITING`, `STATISTICS`, `HISTORY` appear in individual ledger rows 5.25–7.65s; small totals `3.83` and `3.42` appear at 7.10 and 7.65s; `CURRENT CGPA 3.63` resolves 8.00–8.65s.

**Composition:** Pale paper field, wide tilted record ledger occupies lower two-thirds, cropped at left edge. The top-left header sits in a navy strip. Course rows are stacked as alternating pale blue strips; no cards. A cobalt graph line runs along the right side of the ledger, vertically through two large term markers.

**Artistic idea:** The transcript turns into a plotted trajectory: grade rows slide into alignment and their right edges become the points of one continuous line.

**Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.35: incoming node expands and splits into two semester points. 0.35–1.25: paper ledger folds open from its center seam; header snaps into place. 1.10–2.35: course rows assemble in a staggered cascade from left and right; grade letters click into their rightmost cells. 2.35–3.10: point totals tick upward in IBM Plex Mono while the trajectory line draws between terms. 3.10–3.55: a vertical cobalt sweep highlights the completed record; the `CURRENT CGPA` value punches forward. 3.55–4.666: camera pushes toward the last trajectory node; the ledger scales past frame and the circular node becomes a target dial.

**Transition contract:** At 9.333s, the last chart node enlarges and rotates a few degrees to become the center of Shot 03's target dial. A thin chart baseline survives beneath the dial.

**Assets and technique:** CSS ledger geometry; SVG path draw for trajectory; transform-based row assembly; staggered values. No external image.

**Sound intent:** Music continues; the point punch and cut align to the measured 9.333s onset.

### Shot 03 / 9.333–14.000s / 4.667s — “CHOOSE THE DESTINATION.”

**Purpose:** Make the graduation target feel user-defined and grounded in a 4.0 scale.

**Exact content:** `YOUR TARGET` 9.15–10.50s; `3.60 / 4.00` 9.50–13.20s; `120 DEGREE CREDITS` 10.20–11.75s; status `WITHIN REACH` 11.35–13.25s; descriptor `A goal you can plan toward.` 12.00–13.35s.

**Composition:** The paper ledger disappears into a large centered circular dial, occupying 58% of frame height. Navy outer ticks, pale blue segments, and a cobalt arc make the 4.0 scale legible. Target figure sits centered in Instrument Serif, with the scale below in monospaced type. Status tag moves alone along the lower-left safe zone.

**Artistic idea:** A target is a movable endpoint, not a fixed grade: the cobalt arc physically sweeps from current CGPA to the 3.60 mark and reveals how much remains.

**Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.45: node blooms into a ruled circular scale; grid lines bow around its perimeter. 0.30–1.15: ticks unfurl clockwise; current marker settles at 3.18. 1.15–2.00: cobalt arc traces from 3.18 toward the `3.60` endpoint; value increments in three readable steps: `3.28`, `3.44`, `3.60`. 2.00–2.75: `120 DEGREE CREDITS` sweeps into a narrow lower band and locks under the target. 2.75–3.55: status resolves from amber outline to green `WITHIN REACH`; a soft pale-green fill expands only inside the status chip. 3.55–3.90: dial gives a precise 1.02x settle; supporting line resolves. 3.90–4.667: the arc opens at its top and stretches diagonally into a ribbon that carries grade chips into Shot 04.

**Transition contract:** At 14.000s, the arc's terminal cobalt tick exits on a diagonal and becomes the first `A` grade chip entering Shot 04; the pale dial background cuts to a cool-blue field as the grade token crosses.

**Assets and technique:** Animated SVG dial and arc, CSS status chip, number steps driven by `useSceneTimer`; no real brand logo assets provided.

**Sound intent:** Music continues; arc terminal tick and cut align to the measured 14.000s onset.

### Shot 04 / 14.000–19.333s / 5.333s — “WHAT IF NEXT TERM GOES WELL?”

**Purpose:** Demonstrate that upcoming grades can be tested without rewriting completed coursework.

**Exact content:** `WHAT IF NEXT TERM GOES WELL?` 14.12–15.45s; course rows `CELL BIOLOGY` / `A` and `DATA ANALYSIS` / `B+` from 14.70–16.25s; `SCENARIO ONLY` 16.00–17.30s; `PREDICTED CGPA 3.71` 16.60–18.30s; `Completed record stays unchanged.` 17.45–18.45s.

**Composition:** Cool blue field, diagonal token stream from upper-right toward a compact white scenario ledger at lower-left; a large `3.71` occupies right-center. Ledger is angled slightly, with a dashed boundary that visually separates hypothetical work from the immutable record.

**Artistic idea:** Grades behave like weighted pieces being placed on a balance: each chip enters the scenario ledger, and the forecast line tilts and rises without touching the completed record.

**Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.30: incoming grade chip crosses frame, pivots to face camera, then lands in the dashed scenario ledger. 0.30–1.10: headline snaps into two lines with a mild shear; ledger expands from the landing point. 1.10–2.10: `A` and `B+` chips fall at different depths into two course rows; each landing emits a ring that traces a short path to the forecast. 2.10–3.00: predicted CGPA rolls through `3.63 → 3.68 → 3.71`; graph line rises accordingly. 3.00–3.75: `SCENARIO ONLY` stamp swings once and settles; completed record remains visible as a locked navy strip behind. 3.75–4.35: small explanatory line resolves. 4.35–5.333: forecast digits scale toward camera, becoming a circular aperture into Shot 05; ledger and chips peel outward along two different axes.

**Transition contract:** At 19.333s, `3.71` pushes through lens then dims as the next scene's `3.42` forms behind it. The rising line continues across the cut, changing from forecast blue to graduation cobalt.

**Assets and technique:** CSS grade chips and ledger rows; deterministic SVG ring ripples; staged odometer values via `useSceneTimer`. No external imagery.

**Sound intent:** Music continues; token impacts remain visual only. The payoff cut aligns to the measured 19.333s onset.

### Shot 05 / 19.333–24.000s / 4.667s — “A CLEAR PATH TO GRADUATION.”

**Purpose:** Resolve the simulator's central value: the GPA required across remaining credits is made actionable and clear.

**Exact content:** `3.42` appears 19.18–21.85s; label `REQUIRED GPA FROM HERE` 19.45–21.90s; `60 CREDITS REMAINING` 20.15–21.70s; `A clear path to graduation.` 21.10–22.70s; `studyline` and the simple `s.` brand mark 21.50–24.00s.

**Composition:** Clean pale blue field with a single centered white planning surface. The required GPA sits center-left at 23vmin, with the trajectory chart rising from lower-left and resolving into an endpoint just above the value. The Studyline wordmark and `s.` mark sit lower center. Wide negative space and a small green reachability bar contrast with the busy middle.

**Artistic idea:** The ending's graph line completes a path and loops into the typographic `s.` mark; the interface condenses into one legible promise rather than another dashboard view.

**Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.35: push-through resolves from Shot 04's number to a centered paper surface; line enters from lower-left. 0.35–1.10: required `3.42` sharpens, scale 1.15 to 1.00; line draws to its target point. 1.10–1.85: label resolves as one line and `60 CREDITS REMAINING` snaps below; green reachability bar fills behind the point. 1.85–2.65: chart line's end turns into an `s.` monogram stroke; wordmark `studyline` resolves beside it. 2.65–3.55: `A clear path to graduation.` appears below the lockup; one quiet cobalt pulse travels along the finished line. 3.55–4.25: composition contracts into the same circular measuring aperture and target tick from Shot 01. 4.25–4.667: the aperture closes to the opening's cobalt band; final band is already in motion and matches Shot 01's first frame for a seamless loop.

**Transition contract:** The finale is deliberately different from the middle: palette lightens, motion resolves instead of assembling more data. At 24.000s, the outgoing band and aperture match the opening's first cobalt band in position, color, and travel direction.

**Assets and technique:** CSS/SVG path and simple text wordmark; no fabricated logo artwork. Reuse Instrument Serif, DM Sans, IBM Plex Mono.

**Sound intent:** Music resolves at 24.000s, with the loop handoff paced to return cleanly to the opener. No VO or SFX requested.

## Variety and composition review

The five beats alternate asymmetric oversized typography, a wide ledger, a centered circular instrument, a diagonal grade-token simulation, and a resolved end card. Adjacent shots change composition, dominant action, and framing. The hero is the planning data itself, not an empty-field title. Every shot contains at least one event after the first reveal and ends in a carrier-led handoff; no full-frame field is held without subject motion. All copy is explicit and the key readouts remain within a 6% safe inset.
