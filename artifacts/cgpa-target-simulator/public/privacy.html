Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This policy is general information, not legal advice. Please have a qualified Ghanaian professional review it for your circumstances.

This policy describes how Studyline, based in Ghana, handles information when you use its CGPA planner and related pages.

Information we handle

  • Account: your email address and authentication details are handled through Supabase Auth. Studyline does not receive or store your account password in readable form.
  • Academic plan: course names, credits, grades, semesters, targets, and settings are saved in browser storage on your device. The planner does not send those course plans to Studyline’s server.
  • Payment and access: Paystack processes the payment. Studyline stores the account association, email, transaction reference, amount, currency, and payment status needed to verify payment and provide access. Studyline does not store full card numbers or card security codes.
  • Technical requests: the hosting provider may process basic connection data needed to deliver and secure the site. Studyline does not intentionally use advertising or analytics trackers.

How we use information

We use account and transaction information to authenticate you, verify payment, provide access, respond to support requests, protect the service, and meet applicable legal obligations. Browser storage keeps your plan and sign-in session available on your device.

Service providers

  • Supabase: account authentication and storage of payment/access records.
  • Paystack: payment processing and transaction verification.
  • Vercel: hosting and delivery of the CGPA planner; it may process technical request logs.
  • Google Fonts: font delivery on the Studyline information website; your browser may connect to Google to retrieve those fonts.

These providers handle information under their own terms and privacy notices. Some processing may take place outside Ghana.

Storage, security, and retention

Academic plans remain in browser storage on the device where you use the planner; clear that browser’s site data to remove them. Account and payment records are retained as needed to provide and verify access, address disputes, and meet legal obligations. We use reasonable safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and requests

You can edit or remove locally saved plans in the planner or clear the browser’s site data. For questions or requests to access, correct, or delete account information, email miahprincejerome50@gmail.com. Some payment records may need to be retained for access verification or legal obligations.

Children

Studyline is intended for people aged 13 and older. If you believe a child under 13 has provided account information, contact us so we can review and address it.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will post the current version here with a revised date. Questions or privacy requests: miahprincejerome50@gmail.com. We handle personal information in accordance with applicable Ghanaian data protection requirements.
